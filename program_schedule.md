# The 13-Week PR Tracker - Program 2.0 Full Schedule

Based on official `Chiko_Lifts_Program_2.0_Formatted FINAL.xlsx`.

## Core Program 2.0 Architecture

- **Program Maxes:** All working loads and percentages calculate directly from the user's entered maxes and round to the nearest 5 lb. All working loads round to nearest 5 lb.
- **Squat Top Singles:** Updated per block to 85–87% (Weeks 1–4) or 90–92% (Weeks 6–9), followed by volume back-off sets.
- **1.5-Rep Deadlift (knee height):** Resets every rep; lower to just below knee, return to lockout, then lower fully. Starts at 60% of estimated true max (66.7%) and adds +5 lb/week when clean and ≤RPE 7. Deload: 2x3 @ 55% true max.
- **Secondary Squat:** Weeks 1–6 Paused Squat starts at 70% and adds +5 lb/week; Week 5 deload is 55%; Week 6 resumes at base +20 lb. Weeks 7–12 High-Bar Squat starts at 75% and adds +5 lb/week; Week 10 deload is 60%; Week 11 resumes at base +15 lb; Week 12 is an easy 60% taper.
- **Bent-Over Row:** 4 strict sets of 4–6 on Day 5. Add 5 lb next exposure only when every set is clean and ≤RPE 8. No failure.
- **Week 13 Testing Window:** 8-day layout with dedicated PR attempt sessions for Bench, Squat, and Deadlift separated by structured full rest days.

## Week 1: Accumulation

### Day 1
*Notes: Monday — Squat*

> **Coaching Rationale:** The cycle opens with a top single at 85%. It's not a max test. It's a smooth, no-grind single at RPE 7.5-8 that gets you used to heavier weight on your back from day one. The 4x4 back-offs at 70% are the real volume work, kept well short of failure so you groove the pattern. Split squats and leg curls build the single-leg stability and hamstring strength that heavier squats will need later.

- **Back squat — top single**: 1 sets x 1 reps, @ 85%, RPE 7.5-8 (*Smooth single, no grind*)
- **Back squat — back-off**: 4 sets x 4 reps, @ 70%, RPE 7
- **Bulgarian split squat**: 3 sets x 10/leg reps
- **Seated leg curl**: 3 sets x 12 reps
- **Pallof press**: 3 sets x 10/side reps
- **Standing calf raise**: 3 sets x 15 reps

### Day 2
*Notes: Tuesday — Bench*

> **Coaching Rationale:** Bench starts with 6x4 at 70%. That's more sets than squat because bench recovers faster and handles volume well. Close-grip bench and triceps work start building lockout strength for the heavier weeks. Face pulls keep the rear delts and rotator cuff balanced against all the pressing.

- **Bench press**: 6 sets x 4 reps, @ 70%, RPE 7
- **Close-grip bench**: 3 sets x 6 reps, RPE 7
- **Incline DB press**: 3 sets x 12 reps
- **Triceps pressdown**: 3 sets x 12 reps
- **Face pull**: 3 sets x 15 reps

### Day 3
*Notes: Thursday — Deadlift*

> **Coaching Rationale:** Deadlift is the lightest main lift relative to your max (68%) because it costs the most recovery. The 1.5-rep deadlift from knee height starts at 60% of your estimated true max (TM ÷ 0.9). Each rep goes lockout → just below the knee → back to lockout → floor, with a reset every rep. This builds strength in the knee-to-lockout range under control. Only add the weekly 5 lb if every rep is clean and RPE 7 or lower.

- **Deadlift**: 3 sets x 4 reps, @ 68%, RPE 7
- **1.5-rep deadlift (knee height)**: 3 sets x 3 reps, 60% est. 1RM (*Reset every rep; lower to just below knee, return to lockout, then lower fully. W1 60% true DL max; add +5 lb/week only if clean and ≤RPE 7. Deload: 2x3 @55% true max.*)
- **Reverse hyper**: 2 sets x 15 reps
- **Jefferson curl**: 2 sets x 8 reps

### Day 4
*Notes: Friday — Bench variant / upper body*

> **Coaching Rationale:** This is a second bench day, not a second heavy bench day. The 3-1-1 tempo at 65% keeps the weight light while you practice bar path and control. Pulldowns and arm work round out the upper body. Rowing has moved to Saturday as a heavy bent-over row.

- **Tempo bench (3-1-1)**: 4 sets x 4 reps, @ 65%, RPE 6-7
- **Lat pulldown**: 3 sets x 12 reps
- **Lateral raise**: 3 sets x 15 reps
- **Curl**: 3 sets x 12 reps
- **Pallof press**: 3 sets x 10/side reps

### Day 5
*Notes: Saturday — Squat variant + Bent-over row*

> **Coaching Rationale:** Paused squat at 70% builds strength in the bottom of the squat. From here it goes up a fixed 5 lb each week instead of following a percentage. The bent-over row replaces the old rack pull slot: 4 strict sets of 4-6, adding 5 lb next time only when every set is clean and RPE 8 or lower. Never row to failure.

- **Paused squat**: 3 sets x 3 reps, 70%, RPE ≤8 (*Clean reps; no grinding*)
- **Bent-over row**: 4 sets x 4-6 reps, RPE progression (*4 strict sets. Add 5 lb next exposure only when every set is clean and ≤RPE 8; deload load/reps, no failure.*)
- **Reverse hyper**: 2 sets x 12 reps
- **GHR or Nordic curl**: 3 sets x 8 reps
- **Jefferson curl**: 1 sets x 8 reps

*Notes: Monday — Squat*

> **Coaching Rationale:** This is the second deload. The top single at 60% is optional, so skip it if anything feels off. Back-offs are 3x2 at 65%, RPE 6. This week clears the fatigue from three weeks of heavy intensification before the peak.

- **Squat — top single**: 1 sets x 1 reps, @ 60%, RPE 7-8 cap (*Skip if slow or technically compromised*)
- **Squat — back-off**: 3 sets x 2 reps, @ 65%, RPE 6 (*Deload*)
- **Split squat**: 2 sets x 6-8/leg reps
- **Leg curl**: 2 sets x 8 reps
- **Copenhagen plank**: 2 sets x 15sec/side reps

### Day 2
*Notes: Tuesday — Bench*

> **Coaching Rationale:** Bench drops to 5x2 at 65%, RPE 6. The intensity drop has the same purpose as the squat deload.

- **Bench**: 5 sets x 2 reps, @ 65%, RPE 6
- **Close-grip bench**: 2 sets x 5 reps
- **Incline DB press**: 2 sets x 8 reps
- **Face pull**: 2 sets x 15 reps
- **Pallof press**: 2 sets x 8/side reps

### Day 3
*Notes: Thursday — Deadlift*

> **Coaching Rationale:** Deadlift drops to 60% for 2x2. The 1.5-rep deadlift drops to 2x3 at 55% of estimated true max, matching the Week 5 deload.

- **Deadlift**: 2 sets x 2 reps, @ 60%, RPE 6
- **1.5-rep deadlift (knee height)**: 2 sets x 3 reps, 55% est. 1RM — deload (*Reset every rep; lower to just below knee, return to lockout, then lower fully. W1 60% true DL max; add +5 lb/week only if clean and ≤RPE 7. Deload: 2x3 @55% true max.*)
- **Reverse hyper**: 2 sets x 10 reps
- **Jefferson curl**: 1 sets x 6 reps

### Day 4
*Notes: Friday — Bench variant / upper body*

> **Coaching Rationale:** Light paused bench at 60% and minimal accessories. The only goal is blood flow and keeping the pattern sharp.

- **Light paused bench**: 3 sets x 2 reps, @ 60%, RPE 6
- **Lat pulldown**: 2 sets x 10 reps
- **Lateral raise**: 2 sets x 12-15 reps
- **Curl**: 2 sets x 10 reps

### Day 5
*Notes: Saturday — Squat variant + Bent-over row*

> **Coaching Rationale:** High-bar squat drops to 2x2 at 60%, and rows are easy. By the end of this week you should feel noticeably fresher. If you don't, consider extra rest before Week 11 instead of pushing into it tired.

- **High-bar squat**: 2 sets x 2 reps, 60% — deload (*Easy deload; reduce fatigue*)
- **Bent-over row**: 4 sets x 4-6 reps, RPE progression (*4 strict sets. Add 5 lb next exposure only when every set is clean and ≤RPE 8; deload load/reps, no failure.*)
- **Reverse hyper**: 1 sets x 10 reps

## Week 11: Peak / Realization

### Day 1
*Notes: Monday — Squat*

> **Coaching Rationale:** Peak week starts with a true heavy single at 90%, RPE 8-9, then 2 crisp back-off singles at 82%, RPE 6-7. The singles keep your nervous system sharp without the fatigue of real volume.

- **Squat — top single**: 1 sets x 1 reps, @ 90%, RPE 8-9
- **Squat — back-off**: 2 sets x 1 reps, @ 82%, RPE 6-7 (*Crisp*)
- **Copenhagen plank**: 2 sets x 15sec/side reps
- **Pallof press**: 2 sets x 8/side reps

### Day 2
*Notes: Tuesday — Bench*

> **Coaching Rationale:** Bench now gets its own top single at 90% (RPE 8-9 cap), then 4 back-off singles at 82%. That's two more back-off sets than the original 2x1 because bench recovers fastest. Stop the back-offs if bar speed drops.

- **Bench — top single**: 1 sets x 1 reps, @ 90%, RPE 8-9 cap
- **Bench — back-off**: 4 sets x 1 reps, @ 82% (*Crisp reps, stop if speed drops*)
- **Close-grip bench**: 2 sets x 3 reps
- **Face pull**: 2 sets x 15 reps

### Day 3
*Notes: Thursday — Deadlift*

> **Coaching Rationale:** One heavy deadlift single at 88%, RPE 8-9. The 1.5-rep deadlift continues at base + 50 lb for 3x3. Hold the weight or cut a set if the single was hard, because test week is close.

- **Deadlift**: 1 sets x 1 reps, @ 88%, RPE 8-9
- **1.5-rep deadlift (knee height)**: 3 sets x 3 reps, 60% est. 1RM + 50 lb (*Reset every rep; lower to just below knee, return to lockout, then lower fully. W1 60% true DL max; add +5 lb/week only if clean and ≤RPE 7. Deload: 2x3 @55% true max.*)
- **Reverse hyper**: 2 sets x 10 reps

### Day 4
*Notes: Friday — Bench variant / upper body*

> **Coaching Rationale:** Competition-style bench at 75%, lighter than Week 9's version. As testing gets closer, every non-peak day should get easier.

- **Competition-style bench**: 2 sets x 2 reps, @ 75%, RPE 7
- **Lat pulldown**: 2 sets x 8-10 reps
- **Lateral raise**: 2 sets x 12-15 reps

### Day 5
*Notes: Saturday — Squat variant + Bent-over row*

> **Coaching Rationale:** High-bar squat resumes progression at base + 15 lb for 2x2. Rows continue, kept clean and well short of failure.

- **High-bar squat**: 2 sets x 2 reps, 75% + 15 lb (resume progression), RPE ≤8 (*Clean reps; no grinding*)
- **Bent-over row**: 4 sets x 4-6 reps, RPE progression (*4 strict sets. Add 5 lb next exposure only when every set is clean and ≤RPE 8; deload load/reps, no failure.*)
- **Reverse hyper**: 1 sets x 10 reps

## Week 12: Taper

### Day 1
*Notes: Monday — Squat*

> **Coaching Rationale:** Taper week. The top single is capped at RPE 7 (80%) and should be crisp only. The 2 back-off singles at 82% are taper volume at RPE 6-7. Note: the back-off singles are prescribed slightly heavier than the top single. Nothing here is a max effort. The goal is to stay sharp while fatigue drops.

- **Squat — top single**: 1 sets x 1 reps, @ 80%, RPE 7 cap (*Crisp only*)
- **Squat — back-off**: 2 sets x 1 reps, @ 82%, RPE 6-7 (*Taper volume*)
- **Pallof press**: 1 sets x 8/side reps

### Day 2
*Notes: Tuesday — Bench*

> **Coaching Rationale:** Bench is 5 singles at 85%, RPE 7-8. Intensity stays high so your nervous system stays sharp, while volume is low enough to arrive at test day fresh.

- **Bench**: 5 sets x 1 reps, @ 85%, RPE 7-8
- **Close-grip bench**: 1 sets x 3 reps (*light*)
- **Face pull**: 1 sets x 15 reps

### Day 3
*Notes: Thursday — Deadlift*

> **Coaching Rationale:** Deadlift is 2 singles at 82%, RPE 7. The 1.5-rep deadlift reaches its last progression step at base + 55 lb. If it's anything above RPE 7, drop the weight. Freshness for test day comes first.

- **Deadlift**: 2 sets x 1 reps, @ 82%, RPE 7
- **1.5-rep deadlift (knee height)**: 3 sets x 3 reps, 60% est. 1RM + 55 lb (*Reset every rep; lower to just below knee, return to lockout, then lower fully. W1 60% true DL max; add +5 lb/week only if clean and ≤RPE 7. Deload: 2x3 @55% true max.*)
- **Reverse hyper**: 1 sets x 10 reps

### Day 4
*Notes: Friday — Bench variant / upper body*

> **Coaching Rationale:** Purely maintenance: light lockout work at 65% and one set of pulldowns. Nothing in this session should feel hard.

- **Bench lockout support**: 2 sets x 3 reps, @ 65%
- **Lat pulldown**: 1 sets x 8 reps

### Day 5
*Notes: Saturday — Squat variant + Bent-over row*

> **Coaching Rationale:** High-bar squat at an easy 60% for 2x3, plus clean rows and reverse hypers. This is a touch, not a training stimulus.

- **High-bar squat**: 2 sets x 3 reps, @ 60%
- **Bent-over row**: 4 sets x 4-6 reps, RPE progression (*4 strict sets. Add 5 lb next exposure only when every set is clean and ≤RPE 8; deload load/reps, no failure.*)
- **Reverse hyper**: 1 sets x 10 reps

## Week 13: Testing

### Day 1
*Notes: Day 1 — Light activation*

> **Coaching Rationale:** Squat and bench at 50%, RPE 5 or lower. This is just enough to wake up the patterns and confirm everything feels normal before the PR attempts.

- **Squat activation**: 2 sets x 3 reps, @ 50%, RPE ≤5 (*Crisp only*)
- **Bench activation**: 2 sets x 3 reps, @ 50%, RPE ≤5 (*Crisp only*)

### Day 2
*Notes: Day 2 — Bench PR test*

> **Coaching Rationale:** Bench is tested first because it recovers fastest. Attempts are based on your current true 1RM, not your TM. Open around 90% of your 1RM and only take the next attempt if the last one was clean.

- **Bench PR attempts**: 3 sets x 1 reps, Opener ~90% 1RM (*Use current true 1RM, not TM. Opener ~90% current 1RM; next attempts only if clean.*)

### Day 3
*Notes: Day 3 — Rest*

> **Coaching Rationale:** Full rest day. No loaded lifting, so squat testing gets at least 48 hours after the bench attempts.

- **No lifting / recovery**: Rest Day — *Full rest; no loaded lifting.*

### Day 4
*Notes: Day 4 — Squat PR test*

> **Coaching Rationale:** Squat PR attempts, based on your current true 1RM, at least 48 hours after bench. The opener shown is about 90% of your 1RM. Don't force a grinder. A clean PR beats a missed attempt.

- **Squat PR attempts**: 3 sets x 1 reps, Opener ~90% 1RM (*Use current true 1RM, not TM. At least 48h after bench; no forced grinder.*)

### Day 5
*Notes: Day 5 — Rest / recovery*

> **Coaching Rationale:** Rest day one of three before deadlift testing. Deadlift needs the most recovery of any lift.

- **No lifting / recovery**: Rest Day — *Full rest; no loaded lifting.*

### Day 6
*Notes: Day 6 — Rest / full recovery*

> **Coaching Rationale:** Rest day two. Full rest, no loaded lifting, so the fatigue from the squat test clears before you pull.

- **No lifting**: Rest Day — *Full rest before deadlift testing.*

### Day 7
*Notes: Day 7 — Rest / full recovery*

> **Coaching Rationale:** Rest day three. This gives well over the minimum 72 hours between squat and deadlift testing.

- **No lifting**: Rest Day — *Full rest before deadlift testing.*

### Day 8
*Notes: Day 8 — Deadlift PR test*

> **Coaching Rationale:** Deadlift PR attempts, the last test of the program. Open around 90% of your current true 1RM and only increase attempts if the last one was clean. The three rest days beforehand mean this number reflects your actual strength, not leftover fatigue from the squat test.

- **Deadlift PR attempts**: 3 sets x 1 reps, Opener ~90% 1RM (*Use current true 1RM, not TM. At least 72h after squat; opener ~90% true 1RM; attempt increases only if clean.*)
